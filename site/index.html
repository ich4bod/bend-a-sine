Each press plays one 0.8-second note. Changing a control stops it; changing a control never plays it. Start with your volume low.

These plots show four carrier cycles. The upper sine changes the angle inside the lower sine; it is not another sound mixed on top.

Zero index leaves a plain sine. More index bends its shape, without turning up its amplitude.

The rule

y(t) = sin(2π × 220 × t + I × sin(2π × M × t)). I is the phase index in radians; M is the modulator frequency in hertz.
